## One Tap Headache Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

One Tap Headache Tracker is a personal logging tool. It does not require
an account and does not upload your headache history to a
developer-owned server.

## Health and device data

When you grant permission, the app may read Apple Health data including
steps, active and basal energy, walking or running distance, exercise,
stand time, flights climbed, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV,
respiratory rate, blood oxygen, VO2 max, walking speed, environmental
audio exposure, mindful minutes, and recent workouts. It reads this data
to add context to local entries. The app does not write HealthKit data.

The app may use your current location when you log an entry to request
weather, air quality, UV, and pollen-style context from Open-Meteo.
Requests include approximate latitude and longitude. The saved entry
keeps resulting context such as temperature, AQI, and locality rather
than raw coordinates. Background requests use the last known coarse
location and do not request continuous or Always location access.

## Storage and sharing

Headache entries, HealthKit-derived context, settings, watch sync data,
and CSV exports are handled locally. CSV files are shared only when you
choose a destination. The app has no account, remote log database,
advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

## Purchases

Pro options are a yearly auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day
introductory trial for eligible accounts, a monthly auto-renewing
subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment.
RevenueCat verifies entitlement status and may process an anonymous app
user ID, purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical
context. It does not receive your headache records, HealthKit data,
location history, or exported files. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Ad attribution

If you install One Tap Headache Tracker from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple's
AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign and keyword led to
the install. This uses no advertising identifier and does not track you
across other apps. We use it only to measure whether our ads pay for
themselves.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app.
You can revoke Health and Location permissions in iOS Settings. Exported
files are controlled by the destination you choose. Apple and RevenueCat
retain transaction records under their policies.

## Children and medical use

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect their information. It is a tracking tool, not medical advice,
diagnosis, emergency service, or treatment.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date
above identifies the current version.
