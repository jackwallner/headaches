One Tap Headache Tracker for Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

One Tap Headache Tracker (published on Google Play by E3 Apps) is a personal headache log for Android. One tap records a headache, and the app adds the time, optional local weather, and optional sleep from Health Connect. It does not require an account, and your headache history is never uploaded to a developer-owned server.

Your headache log

Entries, severity ratings, notes, quiz answers, daily summaries, and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. A CSV export or doctor PDF is created on your phone and shared only with the app you pick in the share sheet. A CSV you import is read on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

Health Connect data

With your permission, the app reads your sleep sessions from Health Connect to note how long you slept before a headache and to compare sleep with your headache days. It reads nothing else and never writes to Health Connect. Sleep data is processed on your phone only. It is not uploaded to any server, is not sold, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising, marketing, or data mining. The app's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time in Android Settings > Health Connect > App permissions > One Tap Headache Tracker.

Location and weather

If you allow approximate location, the app reads a coarse location when you log a headache. It sends those approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo, a public weather service, to fetch temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and air quality for that time and place. With Headache Pro alerts turned on, the app checks the forecast every few hours using the last approximate location saved on your phone; it does not track your location in the background. Your phone's built-in geocoder (part of your Android system services) turns the coordinates into a city name. No headache data, notes, or sleep data are ever sent with these requests. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Headache Pro alerts are generated on your phone from the forecast and your own logs. No notification content is sent to a server.

Purchases

Headache Pro is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchase status and may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, simple counts of when the upgrade screen was shown (for example, how many times and on which screen), and limited technical context such as app version and device type. It never receives your headache log, location, or Health Connect data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review card. Google handles that card; the app does not see your answer.

What we do not collect

No account, advertising, ad identifiers, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, contacts, or photos. We do not sell personal information, and we share it only as described above.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app, which removes it. Open-Meteo does not receive anything that identifies you. Google and RevenueCat keep transaction records as needed to process and restore purchases.

To delete your data from One Tap Headache Tracker (E3 Apps):

  1. Headache entries: delete them in the History tab, or uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > One Tap Headache Tracker > Storage > Clear storage. None of this is stored off your phone.
  2. Health Connect records and permissions: manage them in Android Settings > Health Connect. The app never copies them off your phone.
  3. Data held by RevenueCat for your anonymous purchase ID (purchase history and upgrade-screen counts): email jackwallner+ha@gmail.com with the subject "Headache Tracker Android data deletion". We delete it within 30 days. Google Play order records are kept by Google under its own policy and are not deleted by this request.

Health information

One Tap Headache Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It describes patterns in your own data, is not a medical device, and is not medical advice. Talk to a qualified clinician about your headaches.

Children and changes

One Tap Headache Tracker is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
