Best Headache Tracker App for iPhone

By Jack Wallner · October 12, 2026

Best for a detailed attack diary with a doctor export: Migraine Buddy. Best for weather and pressure forecasts: Pressure Pal. Best for a migraine disability (MIDAS) score: MiG. Best for logging in one tap with weather, sleep and Watch context filled in: Migraine Headache Tracker Log. Relief is a newer option built around forecasts and reminders. Pick by how much you want to type during an attack.

How we compared. We read each app's App Store listing and pulled rating, rating count and update date on October 12, 2026. We also ran Pressure Pal, Relief and Migraine Buddy's first launch to note sign-up, setup and upgrade screens. We did not subscribe to anything. The apps, one by one

Migraine Buddy

The category leader by rating count. Its listing describes attack records with triggers, symptoms and medications, diary and structured exports for doctors, automatic sleep recording, a 7-day weather forecast and an in-app community. Some features, such as attack duration and coaching, are marked Premium. In our test it opened on a full-screen sign-up and login page with no guest option, so expect to make an account before you log anything.

Migraine Tracker & Diary: MiG

MiG logs frequency, duration, severity, triggers, symptoms and aura, and lists a MIDAS score, a pressure forecast with a sensitivity readout, and a diary export. It is the only app here whose listing mentions the MIDAS questionnaire. The listing shows iPhone only, with no Apple Watch app.

Migraine Tracker: Pressure Pal

Pressure Pal puts each logged attack on a pressure curve, with a 24-hour chart, a 7-day forecast, a medication and trigger log, and a trigger checker that needs a few months of data. Logging is a quick two taps. In our test, a 12-screen quiz and plan screens came before the first log, and the app needs your location for the pressure data to be useful.

Relief - Migraine Tracker

Relief logs intensity, duration, symptoms, medications, weather and sleep, adds custom reminders, and describes AI-assisted pattern detection. It is the newest of the group. In our test an 11-question setup and a plan screen came before the app showed a forecast, so check what is available free before you go through it.

Migraine Headache Tracker Log

Jack Wallner's app. One tap on iPhone, Apple Watch, a widget or the Lock Screen logs a headache and attaches weather and barometric pressure, air quality, and Apple Health data such as sleep and heart rate, with no form. Logging, history, CSV export, widgets and the Watch app are free; the full pattern view, alerts and a PDF for your doctor are in Pro. Limits: it does not record how long an attack lasted, has no medication list, and has only free-text notes for triggers. It has 3 ratings, so its score says little yet.

Which should you pick?

  • You want the most complete attack record, with symptoms, medications and doctor exports, and you are fine with an account: Migraine Buddy.
  • You suspect weather and want pressure forecasts next to your log: Pressure Pal, or MiG if you also want the MIDAS score.
  • You want to log in the middle of an attack without typing, and let the app add weather and sleep: Migraine Headache Tracker Log.
  • You want to log without making an account: Migraine Headache Tracker Log, Pressure Pal or Relief, all of which let us in without one.
  • You track durations and medications closely: choose Migraine Buddy or MiG over a one-tap app.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best headache tracker app for iPhone?

It depends on how much detail you want. Migraine Buddy has the most complete attack diary and doctor exports. Pressure Pal focuses on barometric pressure. Migraine Headache Tracker Log logs in one tap and fills in weather and Apple Health data automatically.

Can a headache tracker app diagnose migraines?

No. A tracker records when headaches happened and what was going on around them. A clinician makes any diagnosis, and a log can help that conversation.

Do I need an account for a migraine tracker?

Not always. In our test Migraine Buddy opened on a sign-up page, while Pressure Pal, Relief and Migraine Headache Tracker Log let us in without an account.

Are there free migraine tracker apps?

All five apps in this comparison are free to download and offer paid upgrades, so check what stays free before you commit. Prices vary by region.
