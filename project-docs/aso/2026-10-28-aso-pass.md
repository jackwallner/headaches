# App Store ASO pass and independent review, 2026-10-28

## Decisions

The independent review revised the proposed metadata before upload. Live ranks are proxies for currently published metadata, not proof that a staged edit has taken effect. The previous 14-day US title experiment lost impressions (400 to 81) and installs (5 to 3), so the US title and subtitle remain unchanged.

- **en-US:** Keep the one-token `symptom` to `export` swap. US `headache export` ranked #11; checked symptom combinations ranked #39 or worse. The title and subtitle remain unchanged. Copy retains the free and Pro boundaries and disclaimer.
- **en-GB:** Keep `one,tap,calendar` in place of `pal,buddy,relief`. `one tap headache` ranked #2; tested removed-term combinations ranked #26 or worse. `headache calendar` was #59. The title and subtitle remain unchanged.
- **en-AU:** Keep the descriptive subtitle and keyword changes. `one tap headache` ranked #2. `migraine risk tracker` ranked #13 and `headache risk` #17, so `risk` is retained in keywords. Tested pal, buddy and relief combinations were outside the top 20. The old visible wording implied unsupported migraine risk prediction.
- **en-CA:** Keep the descriptive subtitle and keyword changes. `one tap headache` ranked #2; `migraine forecast` #58, `barometric migraine forecast` #34 and `headache forecast` #37. Forecast remains in the keyword field, preserving that word. Tested pal, buddy and relief combinations were outside the top 20.
- **de-DE:** Keep the descriptive subtitle, but restore `vorhersage`, `log`, `linderung`, `pain`, and `diary` in keywords. `kopfschmerz vorhersage` ranked #4, `luftdruck migräne vorhersage` #6, `migräne vorhersage` #10, `kopfschmerz log` #6, and `kopfschmerz linderung` #7. These words remain available in the same locale, while the visible subtitle no longer promises migraine prediction. `kopfschmerz pain tracker` and `kopfschmerz wetter diary` both ranked #11. Keep `kalender`, supported by autocomplete, although `migräne kalender` was outside the top 184. Tested combinations of removed `gesundheit` ranked #21 or worse. The keyword field has 1 character unused.
- **ja:** Restore the previous keywords including `ホルモン`: `ホルモン 片頭痛` ranked #9 and `ホルモン 頭痛` #13, while replacement `気圧頭痛` ranked #116. Keep the new one-tap subtitle: `頭痛 ワンタップ` ranked #6, while `片頭痛 予測` ranked #15. The prediction wording is not retained: it made an unsupported visible claim, and replacing an existing Japanese keyword would put higher-ranked queries at risk (`花粉 片頭痛` #5, `CSV 片頭痛` #2, and `空気質 片頭痛` #1).
- **es-MX:** Restore the previous keyword field. `dolor cluster` ranked #5 and `dolor cluster tracker` #3 in Mexico. The proposed repeated US `headache,watch` pair had no stronger locale-specific evidence.
- **pt-BR:** Restore the previous keyword field. `dor cluster` ranked #7 and `cluster tracker` #14 in Brazil. The former has a mixed-intent SERP, but the result still warrants retaining the local term without stronger replacement evidence.
- **fr-FR:** Restore the previous keyword field. `douleur cluster` ranked #6 in France. The US `healthkit headache` alternative ranked #17 and does not justify losing that local term.
- **ru:** Restore the previous keyword field. `мигрень cluster` ranked #2 and `мигрень облегчение` #4 in Russia. English relief SERPs did not test the Russian query.
- **ar-SA:** Restore the previous keyword field. `صداع pain` ranked #3 and `صداع diary` #16 in Saudi Arabia. The same English `headache,watch` pair was proposed across multiple US-indexed fields.

The restored secondary fields preserve existing duplicated English fragments across locales. They are not a proven distinct-query expansion. A further optimization requires evidence for complete, distinct English queries and protection of the local combinations above.

The English, German, and Japanese descriptions remove the claim that history filters and breakdowns require Pro: the year filter in `HistoryView` is available without Pro. German phrasing and German/Japanese trial eligibility wording were also corrected. Logging, history, widgets and Watch logging are free; full insights beyond the sample, doctor PDF, and alerts require Pro. The disclaimer remains.

## Evidence and limitations

- Independent [autocomplete checks](2026-10-28-review-autocomplete.json): 110 requests, 10 prefixes per affected locale, with a single worker. A suggestion is demand evidence, not a rank or download count. Saudi Arabic returned no suggestions for these prefixes, which is inconclusive.
- [Live SERP checks](2026-10-28-review-serps.tsv) use Apple Search API, app ID 6762074561, one worker and at least a 1.5-second gap. A 429 triggered a 10-minute wait before a successful retry. These are search-result proxies and can differ from App Store search. Rank alone does not establish search volume or install impact.
- The original [autocomplete evidence](2026-10-28-apple-autocomplete.json) remains available as prior research; its claims must not be read as proof of the final keyword fields.
- Apple lists the additional US metadata localizations in its [localization table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations). Apple does not combine words across locale fields for the coverage assumptions used here.
- The visible subtitle changes should be measured after release. No autocomplete or finite SERP set can prove that every possible top-10 combination has been identified. Forecast terms in keywords do not imply that the app predicts headaches.
