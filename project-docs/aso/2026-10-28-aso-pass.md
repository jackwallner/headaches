# App Store ASO pass, 2026-10-28

## Scope and decisions

Updated App Store metadata for `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-AU`, `en-CA`, `de-DE`, and `ja`. Used additional US-indexed keyword fields in `es-MX`, `ru`, `ar-SA`, `fr-FR`, and `pt-BR`. App names stay unchanged. Subtitles changed only in `en-AU`, `en-CA`, `de-DE`, and `ja`.

The US title `Migraine Headache Tracker Log` and subtitle `Barometric Pressure Forecast` remain. Commit `496e5bb` reverted the earlier title/subtitle experiment after a matched 14-day result showed impressions falling from 400 to 81 and installs from 5 to 3; the earlier baseline had 345 to 733 impressions and 4 to 8 installs. This conversion evidence outweighs the older rank-only proposal.

Copy leads with one-tap logging and available entry context. Proactive Alerts are described as informational and not as headache prediction. Descriptions retain factual features, free-tier boundaries, subscription terms, privacy links, and health disclaimers.

## Research method and limits

- Apple MZSearchHints: 707 prefix lookups with up to three workers and no errors. Country, prefix, and suggestion output is preserved in [the autocomplete evidence file](2026-10-28-apple-autocomplete.json).
- Apple’s current [App Store localization table](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations) lists US metadata locales Arabic, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, French, Korean, Portuguese Brazil, Russian, Spanish Mexico, and Vietnamese.
- Direct live ranks used `https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=...&country=...&entity=software&limit=200&lang=...`, matching app ID `6762074561`. Some GB, AU, CA, and DE requests returned HTTP 403 or 429. Only successful ranks are reported.
- The local ASO Studio native-search cache was read without refresh for broader US coverage. The table below lists each monitored US query whose latest recorded position was 50 or better, including its observation date. Most are 2026-10-28; each row shows its own date. Sources can disagree on the same day: `one tap migraine` was #1 in the direct API and #3 in the cached tracker, so the tables keep source labels instead of merging ranks.
- German competitor metadata was checked on official App Store listings. German direct SERPs were blocked, so local rank gaps are explicit.

### Term decisions and winnability

- Kept the US title and subtitle because the matched conversion test above showed a clear loss from the earlier swap. Their terms and existing top ranks remain protected.
- Changed the US keyword `symptom` to `export`. The live direct SERP put `headache symptom tracker` at #86; the latest native cache had `migraine symptoms` #102, `headache symptoms tracker` #113, and `symptom diary` outside the first 250. By comparison, `headache export` was #22 on 2026-10-28. The field still keeps `pain`, `diary`, `journal`, `aura`, `cluster`, `tension`, `trigger`, `simple`, `weather`, `calendar`, and `barometer`.
- Apple autocomplete returned bare US queries for `migraine tracker`, `headache tracker`, `headache log`, `migraine log`, `headache diary`, `migraine diary`, `headache journal`, `migraine calendar`, `headache calendar`, `simple headache log`, `headache app`, `migraine app`, and `migraine weather`. App-seeded phrases included `one tap headache diary`, `barometric headache forecast`, `pressure & headache forecast`, `migraine triggers`, and `migraine relief`.
- `one tap headache` was #2; One Tap Headache Diary had 0 ratings and our listing had 3. `cluster headache tracker` was #5; several top results had 1, 3, and 5 ratings, beside Migraine Buddy at 42,251. `pressure headache` was #9; Barometric Headache Forecast had 2 ratings and Pressure & Headache Forecast had 0. `simple headache log` and `barometric headache` were #11, each with low-rating results just ahead. These are the strongest top-10 opportunities in the tested US SERPs.
- Head terms remain difficult: `migraine tracker` was #80. No title change is justified from that rank alone.

- `zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`, and `ko` keep their native-language fields. `vi` is unchanged because there was no Vietnamese storefront autocomplete or phrase-rank evidence in the available source set to justify replacing its terms.
