Log a headache in one tap on iPhone or Apple Watch. Available weather and optional Apple Health context can be saved with each entry.

Review your headache history over time. Add a severity rating and notes, then export a clear record to share with your doctor, GP or migraine specialist.

EACH ENTRY CAN INCLUDE:

- Date, time, weekday, time zone and part of day
- Apple Health data, if you grant access: steps, activity, sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, SpO₂, VO₂ max, respiratory rate, audio exposure, mindful minutes and recent workouts
- Local weather and environmental context, when available: temperature, barometric pressure, humidity, air quality (AQI), UV index and pollen estimates
- An optional severity rating from 1 to 10 and notes about medication, aura, pain location or suspected triggers

HEADACHE PRO

Headache Pro shows descriptive patterns in the entries you record. These are observations in your data, not medical conclusions.

- Personalised pattern insights across sleep, pressure changes, time of day, weekday, HRV, weather and other recorded context, shown with charts and plain explanations
- More than 15 pattern detectors plus analysis of factors recorded together, such as a pressure change and sleep
- Proactive Alerts compare your local forecast with patterns in your past entries. They can notify you when forecast conditions resemble those patterns. Alerts are informational and do not predict a headache
- Personal thresholds and quiet hours let you choose when alerts arrive

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE

- Log from your Apple Watch and sync entries to your paired iPhone
- Use Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets to log without opening the app
- Create Siri Shortcuts, including a shortcut to log a headache
- Export a CSV record to share with your doctor, GP or migraine specialist

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Migraine Tracker- Headache Log stores your log on your device.

- No account required
- No ads or analytics SDKs
- No cloud sync
- Entries stay on your device unless you export them

One-tap logging, history, widgets and Apple Watch logging are free. The full Insights view beyond its free sample, doctor-report PDF export and Proactive Alerts require Headache Pro.

This app is for tracking and information. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent medical conditions. Patterns and alerts do not replace medical advice. Speak with a healthcare professional about medical concerns.
